Our Story

How we started and where we're going

Our humble beginning

Founded in 2025 by a group of passionate students of BRAC University with the aim of reducing cost of fuel

Our innovation lies in deploying modular micro-refineries that convert rice husks, sugarcane bagasse, and other residues into high-purity bioethanol using enzymatic hydrolysis and fermentation. Each BioNova unit supports local entrepreneurs—especially women and youth—by creating jobs, reducing emissions, and promoting clean energy access in underserved areas.

By integrating circular economy principles and community-driven business models, BioNova aims to cut greenhouse gas emissions by up to 70%, reduce open burning of 1.2 million tons of waste, and generate over 10,000 rural jobs by 2030. We align directly with SDGs 7 (Clean Energy), 8 (Decent Work), 12 (Responsible Consumption), and 13 (Climate Action).
